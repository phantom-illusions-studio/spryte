Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Taremwa Studios (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the game Spryte (the “Game”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you play Spryte.

1. Information We Collect

Spryte is a simple platformer game. We collect only the minimum information necessary:

  • Gameplay data – progress, scores, and in-game settings stored locally on your device.
  • Device information – basic technical data such as operating system version and device type (used only for compatibility and crash reporting if enabled).
  • Xbox / Microsoft account data – if you sign in with an Xbox or Microsoft account, we may receive your gamertag and basic profile information through official Microsoft services.

We do not collect personal information such as your real name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. How We Use Information

We use the limited data we collect to:

  • Save your game progress and settings
  • Improve game performance and fix bugs
  • Enable Xbox features (achievements, cloud saves, etc.) when you are signed in

3. Third-Party Services

Spryte uses official Microsoft / Xbox services. When you use Xbox features, Microsoft’s own privacy policy applies:

https://privacy.microsoft.com/

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs that track users across apps, or any other third-party data collectors.

4. Data Storage & Security

Most of your game data stays on your device. Any data processed through Xbox services is handled by Microsoft under their security practices. We do not store personal data on our own servers.

5. Children’s Privacy

Spryte is a general audience platformer and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

6. Your Choices

You can:

  • Play offline without signing into an Xbox account
  • Clear local save data through the game or your device settings
  • Manage Xbox privacy settings at account.xbox.com

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will always show the latest version. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
